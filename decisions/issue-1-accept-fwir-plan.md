# Accept the portable typed FWIR plan

Faraweave Intermediate Representation (FWIR) is the intended format name, with `.fwir` as a provisional extension. Neither establishes public-format compatibility; encoding and compatibility are deferred to #10 after the interpreter and C/backend cutovers. The accepted acyclic issue graph is #1 through #15, with #9 requiring both #7 and #8. Retired `spec/` and `doc/decisions/` documents are not restored; current issue bodies and this plan define the planning boundary.
